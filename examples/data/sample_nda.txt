MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 March 2025 between Harbourline Analytics Ltd. ("Harbourline") and Kestrel Point LLP ("Kestrel").

WHEREAS, the parties wish to explore a possible data-analytics engagement (the "Purpose");

1. Definitions.
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the Purpose, whether oral or written.
"Representatives" means a party's officers, employees and professional advisers.

2. Obligations.
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it only for the Purpose. A party may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives who need to know it, subject to Section 4.

3. Term.
This Agreement continues for two (2) years from the date above. Either party may terminate it on thirty (30) days' written notice.

4. Exceptions.
The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that is public or that the receiving party already lawfully held.
